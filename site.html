RYAN P Work About Contact

I'm a Computer Engineering student at George Mason University and a designer who works on the Edge between hardware and software. I fabricate PCBs in KiCad, write embedded firmware for ESP32-based MIDI controllers and DMX light rigs, and build full-stack apps. My process for innovation starts with a problem I encounter, whether it is a guitar FX keychain I could take anywhere, a program to visualize and simulate quantum mechanics, or a homelab-hosted AI assistant.

I'm also an Eagle Scout who is devoted to serving my community through programs such as National Youth Leadership Training, a weeklong camp that is youth-led, designed to teach leadership to high-achieving Scouts.

When I'm not coding or camping, you can find me practicing guitar for my next band concert or planning the logistics and AV for it. I like building things that help contribute to the human experience and helping people find themselves, whether it is through music, leadership, or technology.
